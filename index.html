GridLab

Interactive Electrical Engineering Laboratory

GridLab is an educational electrical circuit simulation and analysis platform engineered for interactive laboratory instruction, academic experimentation, and circuit verification. Built with clean web standards and zero third-party runtime dependencies, GridLab provides real-time schematic capture, analytical mathematical solvers, and virtual measurement instruments.

⚡ Simulation Engine

Comprehensive Modified Nodal Analysis (MNA) for DC operating points and AC frequency-domain phasor analysis, coupled with numerical integration for dynamic transient waveforms.

🔬 Virtual Instrumentation

Dual-channel virtual oscilloscope with auto-scaling and crosshairs, integrated ammeters, voltmeters, and single-phase/three-phase wattmeters.

📐 Component Library

Full suite of active and passive components: RLC, ideal & non-ideal op-amps, diodes, multi-winding transformers, three-phase sources, and transducer sensor bridges.

📚 Curated Experiment Gallery

Pre-configured benchmark templates verifying core theorems (Thevenin, Superposition), active filter topologies, and industrial instrumentation circuits.
